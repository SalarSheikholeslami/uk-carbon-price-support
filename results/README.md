# UK Carbon Price Support — Results

## Main figures

- Figure 1(a) and (b): UK and Synthetic UK; UK–synthetic outcome gap.
- Figure 2(a), (b), (c): coal, natural gas and renewable generation shares relative to the average donor pool. **These are descriptive mechanism comparisons, not synthetic-control estimates.**
